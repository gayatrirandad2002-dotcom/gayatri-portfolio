Design Philosophy

What guides my design decisions.

Emotional & Behavioral Friction

People don't abandon apps just because of bad UI; they leave because of how the app makes them feel. I design to remove anxiety, judgment, and cognitive overload.

Calm & Focused Interfaces

Treating human attention with respect. No loud badges, no panic-driven streak counters, and no artificial complexity. Just clear next steps.
