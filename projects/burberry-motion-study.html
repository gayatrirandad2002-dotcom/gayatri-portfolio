01. Prototyping Objective

Fluid Transitions with Smart Animate in Figma

This study explored continuous micro-interactions and smooth state transitions for luxury ecommerce interactions. By aligning layer names and coordinate vectors across keyframes, elements shift naturally on user scroll and drag gestures.

The core challenge was coordinating background radial gradient scales, text opacity shifts, and rotating 3D product elements without causing sudden animation jumps between screen frames.

02. Interaction Mechanics

Coordinate Syncing

Matching object states and layer hierarchies across Smart Animate frames to prevent jarring jumps.

Auto Layout Constraints

Applying dynamic constraints so animations maintain proportional fidelity across viewports.
