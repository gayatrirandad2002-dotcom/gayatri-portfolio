PriceGate

A two-tier price broadcasting system designed to eliminate confidential margin leaks and communication chaos in daily commodity trading.

Role Product Design, UX/UI
Focus Role-Based Workflows
Tools Figma, FigJam
Outcome Zero Margin Leakage
01 · The Problem

Daily commodity prices change constantly, but informal broadcast channels leak margins.

In commodity industries, wholesale rates fluctuate every single morning based on market supply. Traditionally, suppliers broadcast prices over WhatsApp groups, blast lists, or manual phone calls. This introduces significant operational friction: negotiated tiered margins leak to competing buyers, old price lists cause invoice disputes, and owners spend morning hours fielding repetitive price queries.

02 · The Users

Suppliers needing speed and buyers needing instant rate clarity.

The Supplier: Needs to update today's per-ton commodity rate in seconds from a phone, verify who has access, and avoid answering 40 phone calls every morning.

The Buyer: Needs today's verified rate immediately before placing material orders, requiring clean timestamps and zero pricing ambiguity.

03 · The Solution

A strict two-tier permission system with gated access.

I separated the product into two purpose-built role interfaces. The Owner Dashboard provides rapid price broadcasting, audit logs, and one-tap customer approval triage. The Buyer View is a calm, read-only rate card that shows only today's verified numbers with an active timestamp, keeping market data strictly controlled.

04 · The Value

100% pricing certainty and protected business relationships.

No accidental rate leaks, no disputes over outdated PDF price lists, and zero repetitive phone calls. Trade buyers receive instant clarity, and the business owner retains total control over trade secrets.

UX Deep Dive

Core Workflow Decisions

Behavioral Security

Verification Barrier Before Data Exposure

Traditional web portals expose catalogs first and ask questions later. In commodity trading, showing rates to competitors or unvetted parties damages business trust.

PriceGate places an Access Pending gate directly after onboarding. New users cannot see products or prices until the supplier manually verifies their business identity.

Efficiency

10-Second Morning Price Broadcast

Suppliers operate on fast timelines. They do not have time for multi-step enterprise workflows when morning trade opens.

The rate update flow features high-contrast inputs, automatic timestamps, and instant synchronization across all authorized customer devices in real time.

Trust & Auditing

Full Audit History & One-Tap Access Control

To eliminate billing disputes, the system logs past rate changes with clear timestamps. If a customer questions a bill, both parties can check the verified historical log.

Customer access is managed through one-tap status toggles (Active vs. Pending), allowing the supplier to grant or revoke access instantly.

Reflection

What This Project Taught Me

B2B tools do not need to be dense or cluttered to be powerful. Business owners are regular mobile users who value clear, calm interfaces that resolve daily friction in seconds.

Designing PriceGate reinforced the importance of balancing strict data permissions with clean, intuitive mobile ergonomics.
